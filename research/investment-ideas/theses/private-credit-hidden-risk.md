## Claim

Private credit's smooth reported returns understate its real risk because marks are appraisal-based, which matters for anyone sizing it as a low-volatility allocation. _(agent framing, awaiting Noah)_

## Drivers and catalysts

- Appraisal-based marks smooth reported volatility
- Listed BDC discounts to NAV can reveal market doubt about marks

## Risks

- Reported marks may prove accurate if defaults stay low

## Kill criteria (what would prove it wrong)

- Listed BDCs trade at or above NAV through a credit downturn
- Realized losses match reported marks in a stress period
