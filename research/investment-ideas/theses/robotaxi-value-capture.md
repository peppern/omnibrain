## Claim

Driverless ride-hailing could become a large profit pool, and the market may be mispricing which of Alphabet (Waymo), Tesla and Uber captures it. _(agent framing, awaiting Noah)_

## Drivers and catalysts

- Waymo deployments expanding
- Driverless as a possible free option inside Alphabet

## Risks

- Tesla's robotaxi upside may already be priced in
- Regulation and safety incidents could slow deployment

## Kill criteria (what would prove it wrong)

- Robotaxi unit economics stay negative at scale
- Deployment stalls for regulatory reasons
