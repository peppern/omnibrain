## Claim

Markets react strongly to noisy macro statistics and the narratives built on them, so headline prints can move prices more than their information content justifies. _(agent framing, awaiting Noah)_

## Drivers and catalysts

- Large portfolios react to wage and jobs prints within their statistical noise

## Risks

- Hard to turn into a repeatable, timed trade

## Kill criteria (what would prove it wrong)
