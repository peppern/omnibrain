## Claim

AI agents could displace incumbents whose business is selling access to gatekept institutional knowledge, such as legal, medical and personal-finance services, because incumbents struggle to rebuild their products and pricing around agents. _(agent framing, awaiting Noah)_

## Drivers and catalysts

- Cheap access to capable models democratizes institutional knowledge
- Agent-native entrants (e.g. Harvey, Legora, Doctronic) gaining adoption

## Risks

- Regulation and licensing protect incumbents in law and medicine
- Incumbents adopt agents fast enough to keep their customers

## Kill criteria (what would prove it wrong)

- Agent-native entrants fail to take meaningful share from incumbents in these categories over several years
