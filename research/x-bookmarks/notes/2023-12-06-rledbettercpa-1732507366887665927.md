## Main Point

Russell Ledbetter's full thread explains who must file the new FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) report and what's at stake.

## Takeaways

- Almost every small LLC, corporation or partnership must file; there are 23 exemptions, e.g. large operating companies (>20 employees and >$5M revenue), inactive entities, public companies, banks and 501(c)s.
- The filing lists entity details plus name, birthdate, address and ID for each beneficial owner: anyone with substantial control or 25%+ ownership.
- Deadlines: entities formed before 2024 had until 12/31/24, 2024 entities 90 days, 2025+ entities 30 days, and any ownership or control change means re-filing within 30 days.
- Penalties are $500/day up to $10,000, with possible jail; FinCEN says filings won't be public. Not legal advice per the author, and the rules are as of Dec 2023.
