> GitHub has officially gone insane.

> You can now build an entire AI content factory without hiring a single person.

> 1. OpenCreator

> Give Codex a creator workspace to write scripts, generate images, create voiceovers, translate videos, and edit content.
> 
> https://t.co/YFG5tZRhTf

> 2. Social Media Research Skills
> 
> Give Claude Code and Codex skills to find standout posts, study competitors, mine comments, and pull hooks from video transcripts. Powered by ScrapeCreators.

> https://t.co/XOW8tV0URo
> 
> 3. Local Deep Research
> 
> Turn a topic into a research report by searching the web and your documents. Works with local and cloud AI models.
> 
> https://t.co/XVSzkcfXzu
> 
> 4. FLUX.2
> 
> Generate and edit images for thumbnails, social posts, and campaigns. The Klein 4B models come with Apache-licensed weights.
> 
> https://t.co/WiqsJCwkns
> 
> 5. Kokoro
> 
> Turn your scripts into AI voiceovers using a compact, open-weight speech model. Build narration directly into your content pipeline.
> 
> https://t.co/8Ffw7c5mOs
> 
> 6. ACE-Step 1.5
> 
> Generate music from text descriptions, create instrumental tracks, and regenerate sections of audio. Runs locally on supported hardware.
> 
> https://t.co/Rl76BjNO0H
> 
> 7. Short Video Factory
> 
> Turn prompts and your footage into batches of short videos, with AI-written copy, voiceovers, automatic editing, and subtitles.
> 
> https://t.co/6YbGCSwZl2
> 
> 8. AI YouTube Shorts Generator
> 
> Turn long YouTube videos into vertical Shorts with AI-picked highlights, transcription, and automatic cropping.
> 
> https://t.co/kpYkdsZcd9
> 
> 9. Podcastfy
> 
> Turn source material into AI podcast conversations. Customize the dialogue and generate audio in multiple languages.
> 
> https://t.co/RBY68LBify
> 
> 10. Social Media Agent
> 
> Turn a URL into X and LinkedIn posts. Review the drafts, make changes, and schedule them through an AI agent workflow.
> 
> https://t.co/ZFweXXrFzF
