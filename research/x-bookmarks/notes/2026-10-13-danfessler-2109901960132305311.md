## Main Point

Dan Fessler released TrellisWM, a 'fractal' window manager for Apple-silicon Macs that docks, tabs and nests real windows inside each other on a canvas you can zoom into as deep as you like.

## Takeaways

- Drag a window onto another's edge to dock it, or onto its middle to stack them as tabs, even across different apps.
- Docking is a hierarchy: double-click to focus a window or group, step in and out with a shortcut, pinch on the trackpad to zoom, and save views to jump back to.
- Windows nested too small to use turn into app icons or tiles; 'Float All Windows' returns you to a normal Mac.
- It's a native AppKit app for macOS 14+. The free tier does dock, nest and zoom; Pro adds tab groups, saved views and URL commands; there's a one-time purchase, and a Windows version is planned.
- It grew out of his popular concept video about making an OS fractal.
