## Main Point

Dan Fessler released TrellisWM, a 'fractal' window manager for macOS that nests windows inside each other on an infinitely zoomable canvas.

## Takeaways

- You can tab any windows together, even from different apps, by dragging one onto another, and dock windows into rows and splits.
- Docking is hierarchical: double-click to focus a window or group, and step in and out of levels with a shortcut.
- You can zoom as deep as you like, save 'frames' to jump back to, and pinch on the trackpad to navigate.
- It grew out of a popular concept video ('what happens when you make an OS fractal'); it's downloadable at trelliswm.app, and he's considering a Windows version.
