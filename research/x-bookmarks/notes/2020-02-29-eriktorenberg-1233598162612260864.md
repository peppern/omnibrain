## Takeaways

- 'Desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want'; anger is a hot coal you hold.
- 'If you can't decide, the answer is no.' We rush choices because we evolved in small tribes where options were scarce.
- All the benefits in life come from compound interest, in money, relationships, health and habits, so pick long-term people and projects.
- Naval redefines freedom from 'doing anything' to freedom from reaction and anger; the thread also covers social-media signaling and minority rule.
