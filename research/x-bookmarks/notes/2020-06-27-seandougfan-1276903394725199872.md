## Main Point

Sean Fanning warns that some public SaaS companies' metrics, like '>95% revenue retention', are defined in misleading ways.

## Takeaways

- His newsletter's 'SPF Infinity' analogy: a metric is only as honest as its footnoted definition.
- Revenue retention can mask what is really gross dollar retention.
- CAC-payback math using understated sales and marketing costs and inflated GAAP revenue flatters companies.
- Written the week of the Wirecard fraud news, as a contrast between outright fraud and creative definitions.
