## Main Point

Noah's own post: cash does distinct jobs, so size each one deliberately instead of holding too much or too little.

## Takeaways

- Cash serves as working capital, operating reserves and investing reserves, and also gives psychological safety in crashes.
- Holding too little assumes you can always sell or borrow when needed, which fails in crises.
- Holding too much causes cash drag; be explicit about how much you hold for each reason.
- Same-day credit like a margin loan or HELOC can cut the need for idle working capital.
