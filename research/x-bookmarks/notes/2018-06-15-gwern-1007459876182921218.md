## Main Point

Gwern recommends two essays: 'Commoditize Your Complement', which he calls the key Silicon Valley economic pattern, and a Gene Wolfe story on freedom and language.

## Takeaways

- Joel Spolsky's 2002 idea: companies try to dominate one layer of a product stack while making adjacent layers cheap commodities.
- That captures most of the consumer surplus and is an alternative to vertical integration.
- Gwern's essay collects many tech examples of the pattern.
- The Wolfe piece ('The Just Man', from The Citadel of the Autarch) is philosophical fiction about freedom and language.
