## Main Point

A study found that more sugar during pregnancy and early childhood, especially from sugary drinks, was linked to worse child memory and learning.

## Takeaways

- Published in the American Journal of Preventive Medicine.
- Swapping in diet soda during pregnancy also seemed to have negative effects.
- Children's fruit intake was linked to higher cognitive scores.
- The advice: limit added sugars during pregnancy and early childhood.

## Original Post
