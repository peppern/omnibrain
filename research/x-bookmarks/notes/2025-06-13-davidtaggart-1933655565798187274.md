## Main Point

A Fundamental Edge × Perplexity webinar shows Perplexity used live for institutional-style equity research (transcribed from the YouTube video).

## Takeaways

- Fundamental Edge contrasts the roughly 6 minutes retail investors spend researching a stock with 30–100 hours for institutional, thesis-driven work.
- The demo builds a Perplexity 'Space' with a master prompt and uploaded SEC filings, earnings transcripts and finance data on the robotaxi industry (Tesla, Uber, Waymo).
- They estimate the market live: Waymo has about 22% of SF taxi share in 15 months, Uber about 6M drivers, giving a possible driverless fleet of 40–50M in 10 years.
- Perplexity says its goal is a near-Bloomberg terminal for everyone, with more data sources coming; the hosts say a similar test nine months earlier wouldn't have produced useful output.
