## Main Point

FIRE's Nadine Strossen explains the 'Weimar fallacy': Weimar Germany had strict hate-speech laws, and censoring the Nazis amplified them (from the video transcript).

## Takeaways

- The fallacy says Hitler would have been stopped if Germany had censored hate speech.
- In fact Weimar had many laws against hate speech, religious insult and incitement, and they were strictly enforced, partly through a large Jewish defense organization.
- Leading Nazis were prosecuted and jailed, their papers suppressed, and Hitler banned from public speaking for years.
- Strossen and historians say the net effect was to give the Nazis attention and sympathy they wouldn't otherwise have had.
