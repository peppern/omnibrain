## Main Point

Two screenshots of Jeff Bezos's shareholder letter on high standards: they're teachable, domain-specific, must be recognized, and need realistic expectations of scope.

## Takeaways

- Teachable: people pick up high standards through exposure, and both high and low standards are 'contagious' on a team.
- Domain-specific: Bezos had high standards for invention and customer care but had to learn them for operations; this keeps you humble about blind spots.
- Recognition and scope: like the handstand coach who says it takes six months, not two weeks, a great six-page memo takes a week or more, not a day or two.
- Skill isn't required of everyone, since someone on the team can have it, and high standards help recruiting and protect the 'invisible' work.
- Mohapatra's point: summarizing what resists summary is critical, because 'the world will forget you, except what you've written down... well.' (Source: images read with vision.)
