## Main Point

Peter Lynch, at the opening of 'Beating the Street', explains why he left Fidelity Magellan at 46 to spend time with his family.

## Takeaways

- He left on May 31, 1990, 13 years to the day after taking the job, having bought more than 15,000 stocks for Magellan.
- He was spending more time with Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac than with his children, who 'almost had to introduce themselves' on weekends.
- At his 46th birthday he realized his father had died at 46, which prompted the decision.
- 'Nobody on his deathbed ever said: I wish I'd spent more time at the office.' (Source: book-page screenshots read with vision.)
