## Main Point

Patrick Collison lists the books he thinks cover the major ideas influential in Silicon Valley (not a 'should-read' list).

## Takeaways

- Tech history: The Dream Machine, Dealers of Lightning, Skunk Works, Masters of Doom, The Making of the Atomic Bomb.
- Ideas: Seeing Like a State, The Beginning of Infinity, The Sovereign Individual, Finite and Infinite Games, A Pattern Language.
- Building: The Mythical Man-Month, SICP, Zero to One, The Hard Thing About Hard Things, The Lean Startup.
- Plus online canons: PG's essays, LessWrong, Slate Star Codex, Marginal Revolution.
