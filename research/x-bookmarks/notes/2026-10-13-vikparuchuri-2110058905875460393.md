## Main Point

Vik Paruchuri announced Indra, a 1M-parameter OCR model from Datalab that is much faster than common open-source OCR tools.

## Takeaways

- 49.4 pages/s on 32 CPU cores vs 4.3 for PP-OCRv6 small and 2.9 for Tesseract 5; about 228 ms per page single-threaded.
- An opt-in --math mode beats PaddleOCR PP-StructureV3 on tables, layout, text and overall OmniParseBench scores (overall 66.8 vs 58.4).
- It outputs every block labeled and boxed, and handles pages scanned upside down.
- Open source: github.com/datalab-to/indra, Hugging Face datalab-to/indra-ocr, pip install indra-ocr. (Video frames read with vision.)
