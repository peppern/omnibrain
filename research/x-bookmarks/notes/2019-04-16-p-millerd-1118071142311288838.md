## Main Point

Paul Millerd's thread (recovered in full) shows that the '40% of Americans can't cover $400' headline misreads the Fed survey.

## Takeaways

- The headline counts people who would put the expense on a card and pay it off at month-end as 'can't cover'; adding them to cash payers gets close to 80%.
- A more direct survey question shows only about 12–15% couldn't cover a $400 expense at all.
- The same report says the share finding it difficult to get by fell to 7% in 2017, about half the 2013 level.
- His point: read the source, because nuanced 'better than before' findings don't get clicks.
