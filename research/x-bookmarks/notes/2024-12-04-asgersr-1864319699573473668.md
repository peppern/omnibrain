## Takeaways

- #1 ATG split squat: strengthens and lengthens knees and hip flexors; 2–3 x 10–20 reps per leg (elevate the front foot to make it easier).
- #2 Jefferson curl: strengthens and lengthens hamstrings and back; 2–3 x 5–15 reps (a bag of heavy books can replace dumbbells).
- #3 DB pullover: upper-body work to prevent the shoulder, back and neck injuries common in runners; 2–3 x 5–15 reps.
- #4 Couch stretch for tight hip flexors, 2 x 30–45 s per leg (more upright means a more intense stretch); #5 heel-elevated squat for knee pain, progressing from assisted to bodyweight to loaded, 2–3 x 10–30 reps.
- It took him about 3 years to run pain-free; the thread ends by promoting a 4-week running program.
