## Main Point

Colin Percival argues that much measured wealth inequality is just demographics: people at different life stages hold very different wealth.

## Takeaways

- His 'Equalland' thought experiment: everyone has an identical life (student loans, same salary, saving for retirement).
- Even with identical lives, a wealth snapshot shows big inequality, because young borrowers have negative net worth and near-retirees have their peak savings.
- So headline inequality stats can overstate real lifetime inequality.
