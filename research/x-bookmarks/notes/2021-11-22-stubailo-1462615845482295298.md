## Main Point

Sashko Stubailo shows how he turns email newsletters into MP3s to listen to while walking or running.

## Takeaways

- The pipeline: Gmail forwards the newsletter (Platformer) to Zapier, which calls an Autocode function.
- Amazon Polly converts the text to speech and saves an MP3 to S3.
- Zapier then delivers the file, giving him audio minutes after each post is published.
