## Main Point

Tall 'mass timber' buildings are fast, low-carbon and surprisingly fire-resistant, but US building codes still cap them at six stories.

## Takeaways

- Cross-laminated timber chars and essentially self-extinguishes after 3–4 hours of fire, while steel fails in about an hour.
- Timber can go up at nearly a floor per week; Minneapolis's T3 was 5x lighter than concrete and steel and framed in 9.5 weeks.
- Timber stores carbon; SOM estimates a 42-story timber tower would emit 78% less carbon than a concrete one.
- The six-story limit comes from an old rule that fire ladders must reach the top floor; tiered height rules were proposed for the 2021 code.
