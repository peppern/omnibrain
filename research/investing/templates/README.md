# Review templates

[Research desk](../README.md)

Copy a template into the corresponding area with a stable ID and a dated revision. Replace placeholders with evidence or an explicit unknown. Templates are not live records and do not count as completed research. Cite source passages, publication/retrieval dates and data periods beside claims; separate facts from assumptions and inference.
