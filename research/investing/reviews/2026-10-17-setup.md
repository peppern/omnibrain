# 2026-10-17 — Workspace setup and inherited leads

**Kind:** setup baseline, not a research run. **Mandate:** [long-term compounding](../mandate.md).

## What exists

The investment-thesis data contract is implemented in Omniscience. [Omnibrain draft PR #2](https://github.com/peppern/omnibrain/pull/2) contains eight candidate theses and nine open, unassigned research tasks. At the reviewed sample revision, each thesis has one source record. [Seed inventory](../theses/README.md).

These candidates predate the business-quality and specific-investment requirements. They have not been screened under the new mandate. No candidate is promoted, rejected or parked by this setup.

## What changed

This desk adds the investment mandate, evidence-routing/review process, reusable templates and separate places for business assessments, expressions, proposal revisions and too-difficult cases. Omniscience epic `omni-83b` tracks implementation, including reuse of the existing worker/scheduler/library backlog.

## Decisions and inputs to collect

Before an actionable recommendation, establish benchmark/base currency, return hurdle, portfolio holdings/constraints, position limits and funding context. Research can proceed while these remain unknown. A future review should identify which inherited leads merit sharpening into business-specific claims; do not automatically carry their old priorities into the new mandate.

## Next useful demonstration

Take one source through routing and one business through the quality screen, then produce either a specific research proposal with explicit blockers or an evidence-backed reason to watch, pass or park. Include contrary evidence. A successful pilot need not recommend a purchase.
