# Investment mandate

Noah's direction, 2026-10-17: use a Buffett/Munger/Berkshire-influenced approach, emphasizing high-quality businesses that can compound for a very long time, with evidence for that durability and a reason to expect better-than-market returns. Be comfortable putting ideas in the too-difficult bucket.

## Business quality

Investigate an understandable business model; durable customer value and competitive advantage; a long reinvestment runway at attractive incremental returns; normalized cash economics and per-share compounding; balance-sheet resilience; and management incentives, governance and capital allocation. For each, record supporting evidence, contrary evidence and unknowns. Historical returns or a large addressable market alone are insufficient.

Explain how reported earnings translate to owner economics, including maintenance investment, working capital, stock compensation, dilution, leverage and acquisitions. Distinguish organic improvement from financial engineering and cyclical peaks. Avoid universal ratio cutoffs across incomparable businesses.

## Price and opportunity cost

A high-quality business still needs a defensible entry valuation. Compare bull/base/bear total-return cases with an explicitly named broad-market total-return benchmark assumption over the same horizon, currency and cost/tax basis. Show what the price appears to assume, what we believe differently, and the evidence for that difference. Prefer a return case supported by business economics; identify dependence on terminal multiple expansion.

Long public equities are the default. A different portfolio tilt must specify exposure, instrument, target change, funding/offset and why it serves the portfolio. Do not infer holdings or portfolio constraints from a watchlist.

## Successful research outcomes

- **Needs research:** a bounded, answerable question with a source plan and stopping condition.
- **Qualified for valuation:** the business case warrants underwriting the price.
- **Watch price:** an understandable business at an unattractive price; specify an entry condition.
- **Too difficult:** a decision-critical unknown we cannot responsibly underwrite within our competence/budget. Retain the evidence, reason and reopening condition.
- **Outside mandate:** preserve useful context without putting it on the equity shortlist.
- **Rejected:** retain the contrary evidence and rationale.
- **Specific proposal or no action:** name the instrument and action, or the precise condition under which we would reconsider.

These are research dispositions. They do not automatically change an owner's thesis status, conviction or portfolio. A parked held business still receives material-risk alerts. A missed run budget defers work; it does not prove that a business is too difficult.

## Defaults and unresolved configuration

Working research horizon: 5–10 years, configurable. Proposed cadence: daily intake, weekly research/review, quarterly and material-event refresh. These are implementation defaults, not an activated schedule.

Historical NHP holdings with coarse size bands are available in [portfolio context](portfolio/README.md); they do not establish current weights or available capital. Benchmark, base currency, required excess-return cushion, position/concentration limits, current holdings, funding sources, and data-freshness limits remain to be configured. Missing inputs block an actionable recommendation; they do not block useful research. Do not invent a precise return hurdle, probability or position size.

Only Noah changes approved mandate constraints, thesis lifecycle/conviction or records portfolio decisions. Cite his instruction and exact proposal revision. No trade execution is part of this routine.
